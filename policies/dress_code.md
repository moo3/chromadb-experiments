# Dress Code Policy

- General office attire is business casual: collared shirts, blouses, slacks, chinos, skirts, or dresses of professional length are expected.
- Casual Fridays allow employees to wear clean, well-fitting jeans and casual tops, provided the clothing is free of offensive graphics or slogans.
- Client-facing meetings require business professional attire: a suit, blazer with dress shirt, or equivalent formal dress regardless of the day of the week.
- Prohibited items include ripped or frayed jeans, athletic shorts, tank tops, graphic t-shirts with offensive content, and beachwear.
- Footwear must be closed-toe in lab and warehouse areas for safety; sandals and open-toe shoes are only acceptable in standard office environments.
- Accessories such as jewelry and hats should be modest and professional; religious or cultural head coverings are always permitted.
- Remote work attire is flexible, but employees on video calls are expected to wear a clean, presentable top at minimum.
- Seasonal guidelines allow short-sleeve polos and lighter fabrics during summer months from June through August.
- Department-specific exceptions apply: engineering and creative teams may wear casual attire daily, while sales and executive roles follow stricter standards.
- Personal grooming including hygiene, trimmed facial hair, and minimal strong fragrances is expected to maintain a comfortable shared workspace.
