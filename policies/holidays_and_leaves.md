# Holidays and Leaves Policy

- Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly starting from the date of hire.
- Sick leave is granted at 10 days per year and can be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member.
- Parental leave provides 16 weeks of fully paid time off for the primary caregiver and 8 weeks for the secondary caregiver following the birth or adoption of a child.
- Bereavement leave allows up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member and 2 paid days for extended family members.
- Jury duty leave is fully paid for the duration of the service, and employees must submit the official summons to HR at least 3 business days in advance when possible.
- The company observes 11 paid public holidays each year, published every December for the following calendar year.
- Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on January 1.
- PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal and approved by your direct manager before the leave begins.
- Planned leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires a minimum notice of 2 weeks; single-day leave requires at least 24 hours notice when possible.
- Blackout periods apply during the last two weeks of each fiscal quarter, during which PTO is only granted in exceptional circumstances with director approval.
