# Technology Use Policy

- Company-issued laptops and devices must be used primarily for work purposes and kept up to date with the latest security patches pushed by IT.
- Personal device usage (BYOD) is permitted for email and calendar only, and requires enrollment in the company mobile device management (MDM) system.
- Software installation on company devices is restricted to the pre-approved software catalog; any other install requires a ticket to IT with a business justification.
- Data security on all devices includes mandatory full-disk encryption, automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity, and no storage of confidential data on removable media.
- When traveling with company equipment, employees must not leave devices unattended in public spaces and should use privacy screens in airports and cafés.
- VPN is required whenever connecting to company systems from outside the office, including from home, co-working spaces, or while traveling.
- Acceptable use of the internet permits reasonable personal browsing during breaks, but prohibits streaming video, large personal downloads, and any illegal activity.
- Social media during work hours should be limited to short breaks; employees must never post confidential company information or speak on behalf of the company without authorization.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long, unique to company systems, rotated every 90 days, and stored only in the approved company password manager.
- Any lost device, suspected phishing email, or security incident must be reported to the IT security team within 1 hour of discovery by calling the 24/7 hotline.
